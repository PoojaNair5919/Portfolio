About Me

I am transitioning into BI and Analytics Engineering, bringing a strong business foundation shaped by my experience at Flipkart. In my role within the lifestyle division, I worked closely with category managers and operations teams, supporting daily performance reporting, flash deal planning, and cross-functional coordination. This experience gave me a clear understanding of how data is consumed in real business environments — what stakeholders need, where reporting breaks down, and how decisions are actually made.

Building on this foundation, I am developing hands-on expertise in Power BI, SQL, and the Microsoft analytics ecosystem, with a focus on semantic modeling, KPI design, and scalable reporting layers. I am a 3X Microsoft Certified professional (PL-300, DP-600, DP-700) actively seeking BI Developer and Analytics Engineer roles.
